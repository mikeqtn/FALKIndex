# Workshop photographs

All by Lewis Hine for the National Research Project (WPA), Hamilton Watch Company, Lancaster,
Pennsylvania, 1936–37. Public domain, U.S. National Archives, via Wikimedia Commons. Cropped to remove
the scanned film border and resized; no other changes. They show the trade, never a FALK record's watch.
